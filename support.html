FAQ

Why can't I connect to SSH?

  • Confirm the target is a reachable Linux host with SSH enabled.
  • Verify host, username, and credential (password or private key).
  • Check port configuration (default is 22 unless your server uses a custom port).

Why is my widget empty?

  • Run at least one successful check in the app first.
  • Add the widget from Home Screen and choose the desired size.
  • Open the app once after changing Pro state to refresh widget data.

How do I restore Pro purchase?

  • Open the Pro sheet and tap "Restore Purchases".
  • Ensure you are signed in with the same Apple ID used for purchase.
  • If billing failed, request help via Apple Support.

Why are diagnostics different from browser tools?

Network paths vary by device and environment. DNS, SSL chain, and response times can differ from desktop browser measurements.
